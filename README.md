Client Requirements SummaryCore Mission: Design, build, and simulate a star-topology computer network for Leeto Freight & Logistics operating in Mahikeng.Assigned Technical Challenge: Wireless Security (WPA2-PSK hardening; Enterprise optional extension). Implement hardened WPA2-PSK encryption on Wireless Access Points (WAPs) serving warehouse operations and logistics staff.Design Constraint: Reserve capacity and pre-allocate subnetting for a new department to be added next year.Client Change Request (CR5): User numbers grow by 25%. The IP addressing scheme must absorb this expansion without re-numbering.2. Physical Star Topology PlanThe physical arrangement follows a Hierarchical Star Topology. All central routing, switching, and core devices form the central hub, with end-device access points branching out as dedicated spokes.Central Hub Node (SW-Core-01):A central Layer 3/Layer 2 core switch connected directly to edge router RTA-Mahikeng-Core.Serves as the central connection point for all department-specific access switches and local server infrastructure.Spoke Access Switch Nodes:SW-Logistics-01 (Spoke 1): Dedicated star endpoint for warehouse operations, tracking terminals, and wireless access points.SW-Admin-01 (Spoke 2): Dedicated star endpoint for administrative and management workstations.SW-IT-01 (Spoke 3): Connects network administration workstations and core servers (DHCP, DNS).SW-Future-01 (Spoke 4 - Provisioned): Reserved physical switch port/trunk on the central hub reserved for next year's expansion.Cabling & Links:Core Trunk Links: High-speed Gigabit Ethernet links connecting each spoke switch directly to SW-Core-01 in a classic point-to-point star layout.Access Links: Straight-Through Copper Cable (Cat6) connecting individual PCs, printers, and WAPs to their respective department switches.3. Logical Topology PlanLogical traffic is segmented via VLANs running over the star topology trunk links to maintain traffic isolation and security:VLAN 10 — IT Management & Core Infrastructure: Management workstations, servers, switch/router management interfaces.VLAN 20 — Logistics & Operations: Warehouse floor devices, barcode scanners, and hardened WPA2-PSK wireless endpoints.VLAN 30 — Administration & Finance: Office desktops and shared local network printers.VLAN 40 — Drivers & Guest Wireless: Isolated wireless access for delivery drivers and visitors.VLAN 50 — Future Department (Design Constraint): Pre-configured logical VLAN and gateway reserved for next year's new department.4.
